**Codex (OpenAI)** is credited as an AI co-author of the additions in this fork,
developed under the project maintainer's direction.

This fork adds the native VS Code extension, automatic walkthrough updates,
browser/system voiceover, paused canvas navigation, small-screen camera
framing, and extension packaging. These additions build on the original
project; this fork does not claim authorship of the upstream work.
